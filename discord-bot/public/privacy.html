Privacy Policy

Spy Sec Music Discord Bot & Web Audio Player
Last Updated: October 2026 User Privacy First

Summary: We respect your privacy. Spy Sec Music does NOT record voice chats, sell user data, track personal messages, or share any personal identifying information with advertisers.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the Bot or our Web Player, we collect only the minimal data strictly required for the service to function:

  • Discord User Information: Your public Discord User ID, username, and avatar URL when logging into the Web Dashboard via Discord OAuth2 or verifying server membership.
  • Discord Server & Channel Information: Guild IDs, server names, and text/voice channel IDs necessary to join voice channels, stream requested audio, and send announcement embeds to your designated channels.
  • Player Preferences: Your saved audio queues, liked tracks, volume level, and equalizer presets stored in local server storage or browser localStorage.
  • Transient Command Queries: Search queries sent to the bot (e.g., song titles or playlist links) are processed in real-time to retrieve streaming URLs and are not stored permanently.

2. What We NEVER Collect

  • We NEVER record, listen to, or save any voice channel conversations. Voice stream connections are strictly output-only (audio playback).
  • We NEVER read, store, or monitor private messages (DMs) or chat history outside of direct bot commands.
  • We NEVER collect passwords, payment details, or personal contact info.

3. How We Use Collected Data

The information collected is used exclusively to:

  • Connect the bot to your voice channel and stream audio requested by your server members.
  • Post "Now Playing" embeds and interactive playback buttons to your configured announcement channel.
  • Authenticate authorized server members on the Web Dashboard and save personal queue and favorite lists.
  • Maintain operational stability, diagnose technical errors, and optimize streaming bitrate.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties. Any interaction with third-party APIs (such as the Discord API, Spotify Metadata API, or YouTube stream extraction) is conducted solely to fulfill your requested audio playback.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Session tokens expire automatically. User favorites and saved queues remain until deleted by the user. If you wish to remove all stored data related to your user account or Discord server, you can clear your queue/favorites directly in the Web Player or contact us to purge all associated database entries immediately.

6. Security Measures

We employ industry-standard security safeguards. Web Dashboard communications use secure HTTPS transport, and session tokens are generated using cryptographically random byte sequences. Access to administrative controls is restricted to authorized server members.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
